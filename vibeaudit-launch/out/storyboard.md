# vibeaudit.us — 30s launch film

**Client:** Twisted Flame Productions · **Formats:** 16:9 (1920x1080) and 9:16 (1080x1920), 60 fps, ~30s, SFX only
**Story in one line:** a real audit of a real app (nyxxi.io) finds a revenue bug, hands over a paste-ready fix prompt, and the re-check proves it got fixed.

Every on-screen word and number comes from the published nyxxi.io sample audit on vibeaudit.us. Nothing is invented.
